First COBRA Academic Conference

Accounting Research in the New Age of Geopolitics

Thursday, 25 February 2027 University of Mannheim, Mannheim Palace

How do trade barriers, sanctions, global tax competition and geopolitical tension shape corporate reporting, tax and investment? The first academic conference of the Mannheim Center for Corporate Behavior and Regulation Analysis (COBRA) brings together presenters and discussants from MIT, Harvard, Columbia, Wharton, Rochester, Iowa or IESE, ESMT and Mannheim.

The conference is part of the public launch of COBRA at the University of Mannheim.

Organized by

Jannis Bischof
Vice Dean of the Business School and Professor of Accounting, University of Mannheim
